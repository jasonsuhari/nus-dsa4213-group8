Aisyah is a Year 2 mechanical engineering student at NTU. She lives with her nenek in Pasir Ris, and she does most of the cooking and groceries for the two of them. Nenek can't take spicy food anymore, so Aisyah prepares mild dishes like sup ayam, bubur, or steamed fish with ginger. Aisyah got a part-time job at a bubble tea shop at Downtown East, working weekends only. Her brother Irfan is in Tekong for NS and is complaining nonstop. He books out on weekends to keep their grandmother company. Hari Raya is in two weeks, and Aisyah is planning the menu for when relatives come to their flat, which will include rendang (made mild for her nenek), lontong (which is Aisyah's signature dish), sayur lodeh, and ketupat. Nenek insists on making her kuih bangkit for Hari Raya, which Aisyah considers the best and is happy to leave to her since it sounds like her signature. Aisyah wants to take driving lessons at ComfortDelGro Driving Centre in Ubi so she can drive her nenek to her clinic appointments, as the bus is hard on her knees. She plans to pursue a Class 3A auto licence, which is the faster route to getting her licence. Aisyah's semester has started, with classes scheduled from Monday to Thursday. She keeps Friday free for errands and her grandmother's polyclinic visits, and Sunday evening for groceries for the two of them. She also has driving lessons on Wednesday nights from 7 to 9 PM.

Jonathan is a civil engineer with LTA, working on the Cross Island Line. He recently returned from a family holiday to Penang from June 6 to June 12 with his wife, Wen, and their two boys, Ethan, who is 12 years old and obsessed with bugs, and a 9-year-old. The trip was a success, and Ethan now wants to go to Japan next year for an insect museum. Jonathan is terrified of flying and hasn't been on a plane in 10 years. He prefers flight-free travel options for their holidays, considering destinations like Penang by train, as the boys love food. He learned that the ETS train from JB to Butterworth is comfortable, followed by a short ferry ride to Penang. Other options include KL by train, Bintan by ferry, or a cruise. The planned itinerary for Penang included taking the train from JB on Day 1, exploring Georgetown street art on Day 2, visiting Penang Hill on Day 3, and going to the Entopia butterfly farm on Day 4, which the boys loved. For the visit to Entopia, Jonathan booked the morning slot, as the butterflies are most active then. Additionally, work wants to send Jonathan to a tunnelling conference in Munich from October 12 to 16, which is causing him some panic due to his fear of flying. He is considering options such as attending the conference virtually or seeking help through a fear-of-flying course or short-term medication as advised by his doctor. Jonathan is now leaning towards trying the fear-of-flying course, as he has never been to Europe and doesn't want to waste the opportunity, especially since Wen would be disappointed. He jokes that he is the only Singaporean who has never been to Japan and is aware that traveling to Japan without flying is tricky, as the only other option would be a long cruise. He has received advice to book an aisle seat near the front of the plane, practice breathing techniques from his course, and inform the crew about his anxiety, as they are trained to help nervous flyers. Jonathan also needs a packing list for the conference, as it will be cold, with temperatures in Munich expected to be around 5 to 12 degrees Celsius, so he will need a warm jacket, layers, and comfortable walking shoes.
